Privacy Policy

BrainIQ Test · App Dev Studio

Last updated: November 5, 2026

App Dev Studio ("we", "our", or "us") provides the BrainIQ Test mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

BrainIQ Test is designed to provide brain-training and puzzle experiences. The App may store gameplay progress, scores, settings, and preferences locally on your device so that the App can operate and remember your progress.

Do not enter sensitive personal information into the App.

2. How Information Is Used

Information stored by the App is used to provide core features, save your progress, remember preferences, and improve your experience. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App may display advertisements, including ads served by Google AdMob. Advertising providers may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, approximate location, device information, and ad interaction data under their own privacy policies and settings. The availability and exact data practices depend on the SDKs and services enabled in the published version of the App.

Google's information about how it uses data from apps that use its services is available at Google's Partner Sites policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers when required to provide app features, advertising, or comply with legal obligations. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay data and preferences stored locally remain on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Data handled by third-party providers is subject to their retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

BrainIQ Test is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service connected to the App, contact us so we can review the concern.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You can stop further collection by discontinuing use of the App and uninstalling it. You can also manage advertising personalization through your device settings and available Google controls.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
